Smart Slip
Open app
Add to iPhone Home Screen
  1. Use Safari (compass icon). Not Chrome or Messages.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom — the square with the up arrow.
  3. You will see AirDrop, Messages, Mail. Scroll down or tap View More.
  4. Tap Add to Home Screen. It has a plus on a square.
  5. Leave the name Smart Slip. Leave Open as Web App on.
  6. Tap Add. Open it from the home screen after that.
